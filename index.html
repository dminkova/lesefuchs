Reading settings

Waits longer before finishing, accepts up to five syllable pieces per word, and treats one to two unheard words as "please check" instead of "skipped".

Automatic: moves up after two careful readings with 95 % or more confirmed accuracy plus a comprehension score of 75 % or more at the current level. Moves down after two readings below 85 % or two quizzes below 50 %.

Claude API key (optional)

Used for new texts and for checking open answers. Without it, everything else works. Create a key at console.anthropic.com and set a monthly spend limit there. The key is saved only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic.

Words often misheard by the microphone

When you mark a word as correct after the microphone doubted it, it goes on this list and won't be marked "likely wrong" again.

Privacy

  • Chrome's speech recognition sends the microphone audio to Google to turn it into text. Lesefuchs does not record or store audio.
  • Progress, settings and the API key stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to GitHub.
  • With an API key, the text, his answers and the list of misread words go to Anthropic for feedback. No name is sent.
  • The reading results come from automatic speech recognition. They show trends, not a diagnosis of his reading.
